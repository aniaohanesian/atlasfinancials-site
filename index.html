About

Ania Ohanesian

Former Deloitte Auditor
Turned Fractional CFO

I founded Infinite Insights when I realized that I am deeply passionate and naturally skilled in finance & accounting, which happens to be many business owners' worst nightmare. While some people hate the idea of spending their days staring at spreadsheet after spreadsheet, I consider Excel my best friend — and I built a business around it.

I've always been passionate about helping others understand complex topics and breaking them down into simple, digestible components. When I realized that finances are one of the key areas keeping businesses from reaching their full potential, my goal became clear: partner with companies to clear the financial roadblocks and help them get to the next level.

I received a B.A. in Economics and Accounting and a certification in Technology Management from UC Santa Barbara. When I graduated in 2022, I was one of six individuals — and the only woman — to graduate with distinction in the major, placing in the top 1% of my class. During my time at university, I co-founded a startup and was among the top 3 ventures to win the USD V2 Pitch Competition.

After I graduated, I spent three years in Big 4 accounting as a lead senior across private and public engagements. While at Deloitte, I had the opportunity to work with complex clients from diverse industries and sizes, that, although challenging, were instrumental in catapulting my professional growth and the technical depth and financial rigor I carry into every engagement today.

I then moved to the industry side as a Senior Financial Analyst for a diversified manufacturing company, sharpening my financial planning, analysis, and operational skills from the inside. I'm currently completing my MBA at UCLA Anderson, which has expanded both my network and my practice. Today I consult with growth-stage companies as a strategic financial partner — identifying opportunities for growth and leading the execution.
